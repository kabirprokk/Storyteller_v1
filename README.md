# About

Storyteller is a modern storytelling platform designed for writers and readers who value creativity, imagination, and meaningful writing.

Whether you're sharing a short story, a personal journey, poetry, or an original series, Storyteller provides an immersive reading experience with a clean, distraction-free interface.

---

# Features

* ✍️ Publish original stories
* 📚 Read stories from different genres
* ❤️ Like, bookmark and follow your favourite writers
* 💬 Join discussions through comments
* 🔍 Discover stories through search and categories
* 👤 Personal reader and writer profiles
* 🌙 Dark and Light themes
* 📱 Responsive experience across desktop, tablet and mobile
* 🎬 Cinematic homepage experience
* 🔔 Notifications and activity updates
* 🛡️ Community moderation and reporting tools
* 📖 Personal reading library and history
* ⚡ Fast, lightweight browsing experience

---

# Vision

Storyteller was created with one goal:

> Give every writer a place where their stories matter.

From first-time authors to experienced storytellers, everyone deserves a platform focused on creativity rather than algorithms.

---

# Privacy & Safety

Storyteller is designed with user privacy, content integrity and account security in mind. Features are continually improved to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for both readers and writers.
